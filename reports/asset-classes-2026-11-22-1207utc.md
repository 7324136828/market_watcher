### 6. Coverage & Data Gaps

- `direct` = quoted instruments of the class itself · `proxy` = listed stand-ins for an OTC or private market · `model` = computed estimate, not a quote.
- **Real Estate** (`proxy`): Direct property is illiquid and appraisal-priced; the class is watched through listed REITs, REIT ETFs and homebuilders, plus Freddie Mac mortgage rates.
- **Alternative Investments** (`proxy`): Crypto is direct. Private equity, private credit and hedge funds publish no daily prices, so they are watched through listed managers, BDCs and liquid-alternative ETFs. Collectibles and GPU rentals are covered by scripts/fetch_market_data.py.
- **Swaps** (`proxy`): Swap and CDS quotes are dealer/OTC data with no free feed. Interest-rate swaps are watched through par OIS rates implied from the dated SOFR futures strip, Fed-funds futures, and listed funds that hold rate swaps, swaptions or CDS. Currency, equity and total-return swaps are not covered. Rate futures are read from dated contracts only: a continuous front-month series steps to a different month's rate at every roll, which is not a price move.
- **Structured Products & Hybrid Securities** (`proxy`): Bank-issued structured notes have no public quotes. The class is watched through listed funds of hybrids (preferreds, convertibles), defined-outcome and option-income strategies, and securitised credit (MBS, CLO, CMBS, leveraged loans).
- **Annuities** (`model`): Annuities are insurance contracts with no market price or free quote feed. The script estimates actuarially fair life-annuity payout rates from the Treasury curve and a Gompertz mortality law (no insurer loads or credit spread, so not a quote), and watches the listed life/annuity insurers that write them.
